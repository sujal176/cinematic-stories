# Cinematic Stories

Build a modern, cinematic video editing portfolio website for freelance video editor Sujal Sarraf.

Key requirements and sections:
- Design & aesthetic: Dark premium palette (deep blacks, charcoal, subtle metallic/amber accent, clean white type), cinematic visuals, fluid responsive layout, and polished micro-interactions.
- Navbar: Sticky navigation with Sujal Sarraf branding, links to Work, About, Services, Contact, and a "Hire Me" CTA button.
- Hero section: Bold headline "I Turn Raw Footage Into Stories.", subheadline, video/cinematic ambient backdrop, and primary CTAs ("View My Work", "Let's Work Together").
- Featured Work / Portfolio: Filterable category tabs (Short-Form, YouTube, Reels, Social Ads, Cinematic Edits, Motion Graphics), cinematic project cards with thumbnail previews, hover video/glow effects, and interactive detail modals showing the video player, editing techniques, and project outcomes.
- Interactive Before & After comparison slider: Compare raw footage with the final color-graded/edited shot.
- About Me: Profile image placeholder, storytelling philosophy, and an interactive experience/milestones timeline.
- Skills & Tools: Video editing competencies (Color Grading, Sound Design, Motion Graphics, Pacing) and tool badges (Adobe Premiere Pro, After Effects, DaVinci Resolve, CapCut, Photoshop).
- Services: Clean service cards with deliverables and descriptions (YouTube Editing, Short-Form/Reels, Commercials & Ads, Cinematic Edits).
- Testimonials: Client review cards with ratings and quotes.
- Contact section & CTA: Banner CTA ("Have footage? Let's turn it into something worth watching.") leading into a comprehensive contact form (Name, Email, Project Type, Budget range, Message) and social media links (Instagram, YouTube, LinkedIn, Email).
- Minimalist footer with copyright and quick links.
- Modular, easy-to-update mock data for all portfolio pieces, testimonials, and tools.
